How to connect

Complete these steps on the phone with the number you entered.

  1. 01Choose your country code, enter your number and tap Generate pairing code.
  2. 02Open WhatsApp on that phone and go to Settings → Linked devices.
  3. 03Tap Link a device, then choose Link with phone number instead.
  4. 04Enter the code shown here before it expires, then wait for the linked device to connect.
⚠️ Only link accounts you own or have explicit permission to manage. Pairing availability depends on WhatsApp and the hosting connection.
